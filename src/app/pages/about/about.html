Credits

Standing on good shoulders

Data & images

  • PokeAPI Species, types, stats and flavor text, free and open.
  • PokeAPI/sprites The official artwork, collected by the community.
  • jsDelivr The CDN that serves every sprite, fast and cached.

Built with

  • Angular Signals, NgModules and the router doing the heavy lifting.
  • RxJS Requests, debouncing and cancelling stale ones.
  • TypeScript Types that keep a growing app honest.

Also: Sass for the design system, Vitest for the tests and Prettier for tidy code.
